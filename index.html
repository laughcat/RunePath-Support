Common questions

Everything important, without making you search through a manual.

How do I play?

Connect adjacent numbers so their total exactly matches the target. A correct path enables Strike. Defeat five enemies and choose one lasting sigil after each battle.

Does using a hint cost anything?

No. Hints are unlimited and free. Tap the lightbulb to reveal one valid route for the current target.

Where is my progress stored?

Best score, completed runs, furthest trial, and haptic preference are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data.

How do I reset the game?

Start a new journey to reset the current run. To erase all saved statistics, delete RunePath and reinstall it.

Does RunePath need an account or internet connection?

No. RunePath has no accounts, ads, purchases, analytics, or online services. The complete game works offline.

Which languages are supported?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, and French.

Still need help?

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your device model, iOS version, app language, and the steps that caused the issue. Do not include private information.
