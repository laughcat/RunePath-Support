Summary

RunePath does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game is designed to work entirely offline.

Information stored on your device

RunePath stores your best score, number of runs, furthest trial, and haptic preference locally using Apple's on-device storage. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Information we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, identifiers, location, contacts, photos, gameplay analytics, diagnostics, or any other personal information.

Network access and third parties

RunePath makes no network requests and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking technology, social login, or third-party data collection SDKs.

Children's privacy

RunePath does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because no personal information is collected, no child profile is created or retained.

This website

This support website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising, forms, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may process standard technical request data under GitHub's own privacy terms when serving the site.

Changes to this policy

If RunePath's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the new practices take effect. The effective date at the top will also be revised.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, open an issue in the RunePath Support repository. Please do not include personal or sensitive information.
